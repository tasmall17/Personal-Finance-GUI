Card Fit

—

What each linked card actually pays you back, checked against what you actually spend on it — not a guess, and not the card's marketing copy. Rates come from each card's own published terms (see the source on each one); anything this app cannot verify from a bank description alone — which channel a flight was booked through, whether a purchase was really at an online retailer — is marked "approximate" rather than stated as fact. A card whose spend is mostly still unclassified in the Inbox will look worse here than it really is, since uncategorised spend can never match a category-based bonus rate; work through the Inbox and this sharpens up.

Bank feed
—

      

        
SimpleFIN is one way to connect a bank without handing this app your login.
          Create a token, paste it once, and this machine pulls every transaction straight into
          the warehouse. The token is claimed by the local server and written to
          ~/.config/flightdeck/simplefin.env with owner-only permissions —
          it is never kept in the browser and never shown again.

        

          
          
        

        

        
A setup token can only be claimed once. Get a fresh
          one from 
          beta-bridge.simplefin.org — do not reuse the one your Firefly importer already holds.

Connect a bank

      
Plaid can only be asked for history once, when the connection is
        first made. Raising it later means disconnecting and starting over, so choose now.
        Two years is the maximum Plaid allows, and many banks offer less.

      
      
      
24 months

      
You will need a Plaid client ID and production secret in
        ~/.config/flightdeck/plaid.env, and your redirect URI registered in their
        dashboard. The README has both steps.
